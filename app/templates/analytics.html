🧠 Dynamic Trend & Velocity Anomaly Model

The AI model continuously learns natural environmental trends while tracking short-term velocity vectors:

  • Adaptive Drift Learning (EWMA): Gradually learns normal diurnal weather cycles (e.g., slow temperature increases during midday) using Exponentially Weighted Moving Averages.
  • Rate-of-Change Spikes ($\Delta / \Delta t$): Tracks rate of change per minute. If a reading spikes drastically faster than historical distribution limits ($>3\sigma$), it is immediately flagged as an abnormality.
  • Isolation Forest: Detects rare non-linear correlations across 3D feature spaces (Temperature, Humidity, RSSI).
